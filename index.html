About KYU Radio

KYU Radio is the student-led voice of Kyambogo University, connecting the campus through music, conversations, interviews, and stories from the university community.

Broadcasting from campus, the station creates space for student voices and the ideas, culture, and everyday life that shape Kyambogo.

What you’ll hear on KYU Radio

Music & culture

Music and campus culture, shaped by the Kyambogo community.

Campus conversations

Interviews and discussions about ideas and life across the university.
