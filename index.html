About KYU Radio

KYU Radio is the student-run voice of our university — broadcasting live from campus, 24 hours a day. We're a training ground for the next generation of broadcasters, producers, and media makers.

All shows are hosted by students, for students. Tune in between lectures, in the library, or anywhere on campus.

Get Involved

Want to host your own show, join the tech team, or help run the station? We're always looking for new voices — no experience needed.
