About KYU Radio

KYU Radio is the student-run voice of our university — broadcasting live from campus, 24 hours a day. We're a training ground for the next generation of broadcasters, producers, and media makers, and a community hub for music, culture, and campus conversation.

Whether you're studying in the library, between lectures, or just need something good to listen to, we're here. All shows are hosted by students, for students.

Get Involved

Want to host your own show, join the tech team, or help run the station? We're always looking for new voices. No experience needed — we'll train you.

Applications open each semester. Reach out to us on social media or email the team to find out when the next intake is.
